How it works

  1. Data — UCI Parkinson's Disease Classification: 756 sustained-phonation recordings from 252 subjects (188 PD, 64 healthy), 753 acoustic features.
  2. Preprocessing — standardisation fitted on the training subjects only; splits grouped by subject to prevent leakage across a subject's three recordings.
  3. Models — a scikit-learn baseline suite (logistic regression, random forest, RBF-SVM), a PyTorch MLP baseline, and a group-attention network that encodes each acoustic family (jitter/shimmer, MFCC, wavelet, TQWT, ...) and fuses them by attention.
  4. Explainability — attention weights say which acoustic family drove a prediction; SHAP gradient attributions say which individual features did.

Research demonstration only — not a medical device and not for diagnostic use.
